01 / Product

That tool you keep wishing for? Build it this afternoon.

Every team has an app it wishes it had: too specific to buy, too small to hire someone for. ElectroPlate closes that gap. Describe what it should do and you get a working desktop app, with its code in a folder you own — ready to use, change, or hand to a developer.

02 / Workflow

  1. 01 Plain English

    Describe it

    Name the app and say what it should do, the way you would explain it to a friend. Pick a look and a data setup, or keep the smart defaults.

  2. 02 Live build

    Watch it come together

    ElectroPlate writes the files, gives them a second pass, installs what they need and opens the app — and every display shows which stage it is on.

  3. 03 Ready to run

    Use it, then keep shaping it

    Your app opens on its own. Ask for a change in plain words and it rebuilds in place, or ship an installer for your machine.

03 / In practice

Two ways to build. One engine underneath.

Easy mode takes a sentence and hands back a running app. Studio opens the hood: live code, the reviewer's notes, every file and the full pipeline log.

Fast by default. Deep when you want it. Start in Easy mode and switch to Studio anytime — same project, same files.

Read a build from across the room.

Every display in ElectroPlate carries a patch of light in the colour of the stage it is in. It moves while the work happens and settles when the app is ready.

04 / Principles

Yours, local, fast.
No strings attached.

  1. 01

    You own every line

    Each app is plain source code in a folder on your computer. Change it, share it, sell it — no lock-in and no per-seat fees.

  2. 02

    Private by default

    Your apps and their data stay on your machine. Your brief goes only to the AI provider you choose, with your own key.

  3. 03

    Checked before you see it

    Every build is launched and clicked through. If it breaks, ElectroPlate repairs it and tries again — up to five times.
